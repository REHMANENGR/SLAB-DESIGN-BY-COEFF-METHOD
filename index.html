Design Summary
Method: ACI Coefficient Method (Method 3) for two-way slabs on stiff supports, m = Lx/Ly between 0.5 and 1.0, interpolated at 0.05 increments across 9 standard edge-continuity cases. For m < 0.5 (b > 2) the slab is designed as one-way. As values shown are the direct result of As = Mu / (3.9·d); OK/FAIL checks additionally verify against ρmin (0.0018 temp. & shrinkage) and max spacing = 2t, same as the source workbook. Positive moments use separate dead-load and live-load coefficient tables (pattern-loading effect); negative moments use the combined factored load. For irregular panels, drop panels, or final construction documents, verify with a full finite-element / yield-line or direct-design-method analysis.
